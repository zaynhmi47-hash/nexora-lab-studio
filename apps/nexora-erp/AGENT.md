# Nexora ERP — AGENT.md

## Concept
Enterprise resource planning layer covering finance, accounting, inventory, purchasing, sales, HR, CRM, and reporting.

## Instructions
- Build around explicit business processes and bounded domains.
- Do not duplicate Finance, HR, CRM, or Identity.
- Preserve traceability across purchasing, inventory, sales, and finance.
- Support organization/tenant isolation.
- Prefer configurable workflows over hard-coded enterprise rules.
