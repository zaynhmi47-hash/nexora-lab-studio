# Nexora Finance — AGENT.md

## Concept
Personal and business finance platform: transactions, income/expense, accounting, budgeting, cashier/POS, bank/e-wallet integrations, QRIS, invoices, receipts, approvals, reconciliation, audit, dashboards, tax, investment tracking, market information, and AI finance/marketing assistance.

## Instructions
- Financial correctness takes priority.
- Preserve transaction history; use void/reversal rather than silent mutation of posted records.
- Validate money, currency, dates, ownership, permissions, and idempotency.
- Keep providers behind adapters.
- Separate market information from financial advice.
- Protect financial and identity data with least privilege and comprehensive audit.
- Keep mobile/web/desktop UX adaptive.
