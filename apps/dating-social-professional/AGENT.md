# Dating / Social-Professional App — AGENT.md

## Concept
Experimental social application combining personal connection and professional/social networking: profiles, discovery, messaging, connections, and relationship-oriented interactions.

## Instructions
- Make privacy, consent, blocking, reporting, and safety foundational.
- Clearly separate personal-connection and professional contexts.
- Never expose private profile data without user-controlled visibility.
- Build moderation and abuse-reporting from the beginning.
- Do not infer sensitive personal attributes for matching without explicit product requirements.
