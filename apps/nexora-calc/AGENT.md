# Nexora Calc / Life Finance — AGENT.md

## Concept
Lightweight financial calculator for installments, interest, savings, investments, tax, profit, and personal-finance calculations.

## Instructions
- Formulas must be deterministic, tested, and documented.
- Distinguish estimates from official financial/tax calculations.
- Handle currency, rounding, percentages, dates, and compounding explicitly.
- Prefer offline-first computation.
- Do not duplicate Nexora Finance's transaction ledger.
