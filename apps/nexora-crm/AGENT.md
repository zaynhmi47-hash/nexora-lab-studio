# Nexora CRM — AGENT.md

## Concept
CRM for customers, leads, contacts, sales pipelines, communications, opportunities, and marketing relationships.

## Instructions
- Model contacts, organizations, leads, opportunities, activities, and pipeline stages separately.
- Keep tenant boundaries and permissions explicit.
- Avoid duplicate customer records where safe identity matching is available.
- Integrate with Finance, Studio, Business Suite, and Social through stable APIs/events.
- Keep automation auditable and reversible.
