# Nexora Creator — AGENT.md

## Concept
Creator identity and monetization layer for teachers, writers, freelancers, artists, and digital creators: profiles, portfolios, publishing, services, content, audiences, and earnings.

## Instructions
- Treat creator identity and ownership as first-class data.
- Separate profile, portfolio, content, services, orders, and earnings.
- Reuse Nexverse, Social, Studio, Finance, and Core capabilities.
- Give creators transparent control over content and monetization.
- Keep analytics privacy-aware.
