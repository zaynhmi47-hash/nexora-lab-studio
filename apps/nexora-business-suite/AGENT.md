# Nexora Business Suite — AGENT.md

## Concept
Business administration workspace for organizations, creators, publishers, and operators: organization setup, administration, finance, CRM, marketing, publishing, learning/campus registration, workflows, and analytics.

## Instructions
- Build around organizations/workspaces and RBAC.
- Reuse Core identity, finance, payments, files, and audit.
- Separate business configuration from platform configuration.
- Handle regional/legal differences through configuration and adapters.
- Sensitive business actions require permission checks and audit events.
- Keep vertical modules enableable per organization.
