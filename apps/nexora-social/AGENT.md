# Nexora Social — AGENT.md

## Concept
General social and professional networking platform: profiles, feeds, connections, messaging, professional identity, portfolios, creator identity, and communities.

## Instructions
- Provide clear privacy controls for profiles, posts, connections, and messages.
- Separate public, private, connection-only, and organization-scoped content.
- Use reusable moderation/reporting primitives.
- Keep professional identity portable through Nexora ID.
- Design for privacy and abuse prevention from the start.
